# Claim scope and limitations
- Proved: Galvin–Zhang Conjecture 1.8 for all n ≥ 0 and ℓ ≥ 1, for both P_n^ℓ and C_n^ℓ. Log-concavity follows as a corollary.
- No extension beyond powers of paths and cycles is claimed.
- Not a first solution: the published Galvin–Zhang paper (Discrete Math. 350(3), 2027, §1) reports that Zheng and Qin proposed an affirmative resolution, by a different approach, while their paper was under review. Their work is not public; this is an independent proof.
- Not refereed; not formally verified.
