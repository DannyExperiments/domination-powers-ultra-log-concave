# Claim scope and limitations
- Proved: Galvin–Zhang Conjecture 1.8 for all n ≥ 0 and ℓ ≥ 1, for both P_n^ℓ and C_n^ℓ. Log-concavity follows as a corollary.
- No extension beyond powers of paths and cycles is claimed.
- Priority uncertain: the published Galvin–Zhang paper (Discrete Math. 350(3), 2027) cites a personal communication by J. Zheng and X. Qin on this exact topic. Its content is unknown to us.
- Not refereed; not formally verified.
