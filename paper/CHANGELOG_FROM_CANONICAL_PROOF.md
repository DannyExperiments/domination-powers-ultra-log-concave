# Changes from the canonical proof note
The mathematics is unchanged. Changes are expository only:
- The inward-pair inequality, convolution closure and comparison lemma are split into Lemmas 2.1–2.3, with explicit index ranges. The comparison is stated as likelihood-ratio order implying stochastic order, with positive totals.
- Section 3 makes the reduction explicit: only pairs with q_k, q_{k+1} > 0 matter, and R_1 = 0.
- Cycles: the merged pair is (g_k, g_1), moved to the front by permutation invariance. The wrap-around distance argument and the conventions for C_1, C_2 and n ≤ 2ℓ+1 are stated.
- Paths: the triple (E_L, d_1, d_2) and the ranges x ∈ [0,ℓ], y ∈ [0,r−1] are named. The final averaging step is written out as an inequality.
- Citations added: Hoggar, Karlin, Stanley, Efron, Shaked–Shanthikumar, Arocha–Llano. The Galvin–Zhang reference is updated to its published version, and its Zheng–Qin personal communication is noted.
