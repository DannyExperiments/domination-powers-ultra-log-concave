# Claim scope and limitations

## Claimed

- For all n ≥ 0 and ℓ ≥ 1, and for G = P_n and G = C_n, the sequence γ_k(G^ℓ)/C(n,k) is log-concave in k. Log-concavity of γ_k(G^ℓ) follows.

## Not claimed

- **Not a first solution.** Galvin and Zhang report an earlier proposed resolution by Zheng and Qin, by a different approach. We have not seen that work.
- No statement beyond powers of paths and cycles.
- No new technique: PF2 convolution, likelihood-ratio order and composition encodings are standard; the deletion-swap comparison is elementary.
- No formal verification. No peer review; the mathematical audits were performed by AI systems.
