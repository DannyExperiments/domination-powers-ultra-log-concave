# Literature and priority audit (through 26 November 2026)

There were three independent search lanes: (1) official status and priority; (2) identical or stronger results; (3) proof ingredients.

## Findings

- **Original problem.** Galvin–Zhang, arXiv:2408.12731v1 (August 2024), Conjecture 1.8. The paper is now published as Discrete Math. 350(3) (2027) 115520, under CC BY 4.0, where the conjecture is numbered 1.7.
- **Prior claim.** In Section 1 of the published version, Galvin and Zhang report that while an earlier version was under review, Zheng and Qin proposed an affirmative resolution by a different approach. Their reference [18] is a personal communication. No public Zheng–Qin preprint was found on arXiv, Semantic Scholar or the web. Classification: **not a first solution**. This repository gives an independent proof, and as far as we know it is the first publicly available one.
- **Identical or stronger published results.** None found. The domination polynomials of these graphs are not real-rooted (checked for n ≤ 25), so Newton's inequalities do not apply. Matroid and Lorentzian methods do not apply either. Log-concavity of bounded-composition counts (Belbachir–Szalay 2008) is weaker.
- **Proof ingredients (all standard).** PF2 closure under convolution (Hoggar 1974; Karlin 1968; Stanley 1989); likelihood-ratio and stochastic orders (Shaked–Shanthikumar 2007); Efron's theorem (1965); composition encodings, due to Arocha–Llano for ℓ = 1. The deletion-swap comparison is elementary.
