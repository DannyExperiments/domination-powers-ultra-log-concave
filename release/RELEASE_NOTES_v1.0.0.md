# Version 1.0.0

**Result.** An independent proof that the domination sequences of all powers of paths and cycles are ultra log-concave (Galvin–Zhang, Discrete Math. 350(3) (2027) 115520, Conjecture 1.7; arXiv Conjecture 1.8).

**Contents.** Four-page manuscript (TeX, PDF, bibliography); proof outline; corroborating exact computations with a positive control and saved logs; summaries of three mathematical audits and a three-lane literature and priority audit; interactive 3D explorer; replay and repository-verification scripts; CI workflows.

**Scope.** Not a first solution: Galvin and Zhang report an earlier proposed resolution by Zheng and Qin (personal communication, not public). As far as we know this is the first publicly available proof. Not formally verified; not peer reviewed. See `CLAIM_SCOPE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`.
